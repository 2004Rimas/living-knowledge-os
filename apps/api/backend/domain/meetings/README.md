# Meetings Domain

Manages meeting data, transcripts, decisions, and action tracking.

## Bounded Context

Responsible for:
- Meeting scheduling and metadata
- Transcript storage and processing
- Speaker and participant tracking
- Decision extraction and documentation
- Action item tracking
- Risk and issue identification

## Aggregates

- **Meeting Aggregate**: Meeting with transcript and extracted data
- **Transcript Aggregate**: Raw and processed transcript data

## Key Entities

### Meeting
- **Purpose**: Scheduled discussion event
- **Attributes**: ID, workspace_id, title, scheduled_at, duration, participants
- **Relationships**: Has Transcript, Decisions, ActionItems, Summaries
- **Rules**: Immutable metadata, soft delete
- **Business Rules**: Records meeting context and participants

### Transcript
- **Purpose**: Recording and transcript of meeting
- **Attributes**: ID, meeting_id, raw_text, processed_text, duration, language
- **Relationships**: Belongs to Meeting, Has Speakers
- **Rules**: Immutable raw transcript

### Speaker
- **Purpose**: Participant in meeting
- **Attributes**: ID, transcript_id, name, email, time_spoken
- **Relationships**: Belongs to Transcript
- **Rules**: Tracks speaking contributions

### Decision
- **Purpose**: Explicit decision made in meeting
- **Attributes**: ID, meeting_id, title, description, date_made, owner_id
- **Relationships**: Belongs to Meeting, Linked to KnowledgeNode
- **Rules**: Linked to knowledge graph
- **Business Rules**: Decision must have owner for accountability

### ActionItem
- **Purpose**: Task assigned during meeting
- **Attributes**: ID, meeting_id, description, assignee_id, due_date, status
- **Relationships**: Belongs to Meeting
- **Rules**: Tracks to Task entity
- **Business Rules**: Status in (Open, In_Progress, Done, Cancelled)

### Risk
- **Purpose**: Identified risks or concerns
- **Attributes**: ID, meeting_id, description, severity, owner_id
- **Relationships**: Belongs to Meeting
- **Rules**: Tracked separately from action items

### Summary
- **Purpose**: AI-generated meeting summary
- **Attributes**: ID, meeting_id, key_points, themes, next_steps
- **Relationships**: Belongs to Meeting
- **Rules**: Generated by Meeting Agent

## Future Extensions
